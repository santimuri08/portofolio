# Brand Archetypes

Based on Jungian archetype theory, adapted for brand positioning
(Margaret Mark & Carol Pearson; March Branding's working set).

The 12 archetypes are shorthand for the emotional stance a brand takes.
Most brands lead with one primary and temper it with one secondary.

## The 12

- **Innocent** — optimism, simplicity, purity (Dove, Coca-Cola)
- **Explorer** — discovery, independence, frontier (Patagonia, Jeep)
- **Sage** — wisdom, truth, expertise (Google, National Geographic)
- **Hero** — courage, mastery, decisive action (Nike, FedEx)
- **Outlaw** — rebellion, disruption, breaking the rules (Harley, Virgin)
- **Magician** — transformation, vision, making the impossible real (Apple, Disney)
- **Regular Guy/Gal** — belonging, relatability, everyman (IKEA, Target)
- **Lover** — intimacy, aesthetic pleasure, connection (Chanel, Victoria's Secret)
- **Jester** — fun, irreverence, play (Old Spice, M&M's)
- **Caregiver** — service, protection, compassion (Johnson & Johnson, UNICEF)
- **Creator** — craft, originality, self-expression (Adobe, Lego)
- **Ruler** — control, excellence, authority (Mercedes, Rolex)

## Portfolio Pairing: Explorer + Creator

**Explorer (primary)** — curiosity, range, willingness to try new tools,
new problems, new domains. Not tied to one stack. Not afraid of the
messy early stage of a product.

**Creator (secondary)** — craft matters. Code is considered. Interfaces
feel deliberate. Output is the point, not the process.

### Why this pair fits the audience

A Series A/B startup hiring manager is looking for someone who can:
- Pick up a new domain fast (Explorer)
- Ship work that feels designed, not assembled (Creator)

Explorer alone reads as "generalist who hasn't committed." Creator alone
reads as "craftsperson who can't move fast." Together they read as
**product engineer**: someone who explores to find the right problem
and creates to ship it well.

### Reference brands in this pair

- **Linear** — Explorer (reinventing project management) + Creator (obsessive UI craft)
- **Vercel** — Explorer (pushing web infrastructure) + Creator (developer experience as art)
- **Raycast** — Explorer (rethinking the launcher) + Creator (tactile, considered UI)
- **Cal.com** — Explorer (open-source alternative) + Creator (scheduling done beautifully)

## Application to This Portfolio

- Copy shows curiosity without bragging (Explorer)
- Layout shows restraint and care (Creator)
- Project range shows both: a tool, a systems project, a playful experiment
- Signature move (calibrated restraint) is the Creator side disciplining the Explorer side

## Anti-patterns

- Leaning too far into Explorer → reads as scattered
- Leaning too far into Creator → reads as precious, slow, portfolio-as-art-piece
- Adding a third archetype → dilutes the signal
