# Spec: Signal (Phase 1)

Who is this site for, what problem do they have, what should they understand
immediately when they land?

## Audience

**Who (specifically):**
A hiring manager at a Series A/B product-quality startup (Linear, Vercel,
Supabase, Raycast, or Cal.com tier). Mid-30s to mid-40s. Has hired before,
has been burned by portfolios that look good but the candidate couldn't ship.

**Situation:**
Sitting with ~40 candidate portfolios open in tabs after posting a role.
Spending 10–20 seconds per tab before closing or bookmarking.
Looking for a reason to bookmark, not a reason to close.

**What they already believe:**
- Most portfolios are generic
- "Passionate about crafting experiences" is a red flag
- Design without shipped code is suspicious; code without design sense is suspicious
