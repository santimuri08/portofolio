## Case study structure (used for every featured project)

Each featured project page contains, in order:

1. **Overview** — one paragraph, what the project is and who it's for
2. **Problem** — what was broken or missing before
3. **Solution** — what I built, at a high level
4. **Tech stack** — what I used and why
5. **My role** — what I personally did (especially on team projects)
6. **Challenges** — one or two real problems and how I solved them
7. **Outcome** — what changed, what I'd do differently
8. **Screenshots** — 3–5 images, captioned
9. **Links** — live site, GitHub
